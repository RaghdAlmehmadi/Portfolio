01 / ABOUT

BEYOND THE DEGREE.

I am an Information Technology graduate from the University of Jeddah, but my relationship with technology extends beyond what I studied in a classroom.

I am naturally curious about how things work, why they work, and — perhaps most importantly — how they could work better.

Throughout my journey, I have explored programming, digital solutions, prototyping, emerging technologies, and innovation. I have built technical projects, participated in national hackathons and innovation programs, and gradually moved from developing projects to creating something of my own.

In 2026, that journey took a new direction when I developed and filed my own patent-pending invention: a non-wearable device designed to detect distraction and provide adaptive intervention.

That invention, Rakeez, went on to win the Gold Medal at the Saudi Global Inventions & Innovations Expo (SGiE 2026), in the Information Technology, AI & Electronics track. 🥇

Alongside my technical and innovation work, I gained professional experience within Saudi Airlines and the Ministry of Energy, where I learned to work within structured environments, collaborate with stakeholders, and translate ideas into practical initiatives.

I am still exploring, still building, and still asking the same question that has followed me throughout my journey:

What if there’s a better way?

That question is where most of my ideas begin.
